## A1. Does a donation count as "Inverkehrbringen"? Which LMG/LGV/HyV duties bind the donating kitchen and the receiving organisation (Selbstkontrolle, Meldepflicht, Rückverfolgbarkeit)?

### Takeaway
Swiss food law applies to gratuitous hand-over of food exactly as to a sale (the Bundesrat's own donation measures — HyV Art. 29 Abs. 3, allergen simplification — only make sense on that premise), and a dedicated federal donation guideline (BLV-Spendenleitfaden, Beretta et al. 2021) plus a ZHAW legal analysis (2024) exist. Article-level text could not be fetched.

### Cited Findings
- The Swiss Hygieneverordnung EDI (HyV, SR 817.024.1, Verordnung des EDI vom 16. Dezember 2016 über die Hygiene beim Umgang mit Lebensmitteln) is the operative hygiene text; the current consolidated version is on Fedlex — [Fedlex SR 817.024.1](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2017/183/de); bilingual mirror [droit-bilingue.ch](https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/fr-de/8/81/817.024.1-index.html); version of 1 Feb 2024 on [lawbrary.ch](https://lawbrary.ch/gesetz/cc/817_024_1/HyV/v2024.02/de/s21/verordnung-des-ediueber-die-hygiene-beim-umgang-mit-lebensmitteln-hygieneverordnung-edi-hyv/mikrobiologische-kriterien-fuer-lebensmittel/).
- In the food-law revision package "Stretto 3", HyV Art. 29 Abs. 3 was supplemented so that retail businesses may deep-freeze meat and meat products in order to resell them or **pass them to donation organisations**; in "Stretto 4" a simplification of allergen declaration for baked goods handed to donation organisations was implemented — [Bericht des Bundesrates in Erfüllung Postulat 22.3882 (parlament.ch PDF)](https://www.parlament.ch/centers/eparl/curia/2022/20223882/Bericht%20BR%20D.pdf) (snippet).
- A federal donation guideline exists: "Leitfaden zur Reduktion von Lebensmittelverlusten bei …" — Beretta et al. 2021, filed as "BLV-Spendenleitfaden" on the BAFU server — [BAFU PDF](https://www.bafu.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/9HPcMiRiuAf0/2021_Beretta-etal_BLV-Spendenleitfaden.pdf). A ZHAW document "Rechtliche Aspekte und Lebensmittelsicherheit" (project data dated 1 Nov 2024) covers the legal side of donations — [ZHAW PDF](https://www.zhaw.ch/forschungsdaten/projectdata/20241101124315_6724bed31b015.pdf); a ZHAW "Leitfaden zur Reduktion von Lebensmittelverlusten" is in the ZHAW digital collection — [ZHAW digitalcollection](https://digitalcollection.zhaw.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/0943a66e-ab7d-4317-9dfd-eeef5a5c56b3/content).
- The working group **FoodSave2025** was initiated by Tischlein deck dich together with partners incl. foodwaste.ch to create the basis for targeted changes to food-law regulations and guidelines so that donations and other forms of food sharing are eased — [foodwaste.ch, Gesetz & Wissenschaft](https://foodwaste.ch/haltbarkeit-lebensmittel/gesetz-wissenschaft/) (snippet).
- Gastronomy businesses "can use surplus food in their kitchens and pass on their own surpluses"; food companies can donate still-consumable surplus which Schweizer Tafel collects and distributes — [GastroSuisse Food waste](https://gastrosuisse.ch/de/branchenwissen/nachhaltigkeit/food-waste) / [Kanton Zürich Nachhaltige Gastronomie](https://www.zh.ch/de/umwelt-tiere/nachhaltige-ernaehrung/nachhaltige-gastronomie.html) (snippets).
- The Kantonales Laboratorium Bern (enforcement authority for the canton) sits at Muesmattstrasse 19, 3012 Bern, tel. +41 31 633 11 11, under the Wirtschafts-, Energie- und Umweltdirektion — [WEU Kanton Bern](https://www.weu.be.ch/de/start/ueber-uns/die-organisation/kantonales-laboratorium.html). Its 2025 inspection round led to five (other report: seven) gastro closures and about 230 Anzeigen in the canton — [plattformj.ch](https://www.plattformj.ch/artikel/240673/); [Berner Zeitung](https://www.bernerzeitung.ch/lebensmittelkontrolle-bern-7-schliessungen-und-230-anzeigen-880255672471); [Der Bund](https://www.derbund.ch/lebensmittelkontrolle-kanton-bern-fast-doppelte-so-viele-anzeigen-291590341342).

### Inferences
- Because the Bundesrat needed to *amend* the HyV (Art. 29 Abs. 3) to permit freezing-for-donation, and needed to *simplify* allergen labelling *for* donation organisations, gratuitous hand-over is plainly treated as regulated "Abgabe"/"Inverkehrbringen" under the LMG — a donating kitchen keeps its full Selbstkontrolle duty and the receiving organisation becomes a food business itself once it stores/re-serves. (Consistent with the known LMG Art. 4 definition, which includes unentgeltliche Weitergabe — **verify wording on Fedlex, SR 817.0, Art. 4 Abs. 1**.)
- A Bern shelter that receives and re-serves meals should expect to be treated by the Kantonales Laboratorium as a Lebensmittelbetrieb (Meldepflicht LMG Art. 11 — **verify**), with its own Selbstkontrolle and traceability records (one step back = the kitchen; one step forward = the serving).

## A2. Temperatures and time limits (hot holding, cooling, cold chain, reheating, maximum time before discard) — HyV articles

### Takeaway
The HyV states the hot-holding duty in general terms (temperatures that prevent multiplication of pathogenic micro-organisms); the numeric values used in Swiss practice (≥ 65 °C hot, ≤ 5 °C chilled, timed cooling) come from the HyV's cold-storage provisions and the sector guideline "Gute Verfahrenspraxis im Gastgewerbe (GVG)". **No numeric value could be verified in this run; do not print figures without the check listed in Gaps.**

### Cited Findings
- "Keeping food warm must take place at temperatures that prevent the multiplication of harmful micro-organisms" — general HyV rule as rendered in search results of the ordinance text — [Fedlex HyV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2017/183/de) / [lexfind PDF of HyV](https://www.lexfind.ch/tolv/232867/de?download=true) (snippet; article number not shown).
- HyV Art. 28 concerns "Zerlege- und Herstellungsbetriebe" (meat cutting/processing establishments), not cooling of cooked food — [Fedlex HyV search snippet](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2017/183/de).
- HyV Art. 29 Abs. 3 (post-Stretto 3) permits retail to deep-freeze meat/meat products for resale or hand-over to donation organisations — [Bericht BR Postulat 22.3882](https://www.parlament.ch/centers/eparl/curia/2022/20223882/Bericht%20BR%20D.pdf) (snippet).
- foodwaste.ch states that most foods with a Mindesthaltbarkeitsdatum can, depending on category, be passed on and consumed 6 to 360 days beyond that date if stored correctly (relevant to packaged goods, not to cooked meals) — [foodwaste.ch](https://foodwaste.ch/haltbarkeit-lebensmittel/gesetz-wissenschaft/) (snippet).

### Inferences
- For *cooked* surplus the binding numbers will be in the HyV chapter on Temperaturen/Lebensmittel (Kühl- und Warmhaltetemperaturen) plus the GVG branch guideline; a same-day platform should design its rules around "hot ≥ 65 °C the whole way, or chilled ≤ 5 °C after a documented rapid cool-down, and a fixed discard deadline on the label" — but the exact thresholds and the cooling window must be copied from the ordinance text, not from this note.

### Gaps
- **Not verified (Fedlex blocked):** the HyV article numbers and wording for (a) Warmhalten ≥ 65 °C, (b) Kühltemperatur for leicht verderbliche Lebensmittel (≤ 5 °C), (c) Abkühlen after cooking (time/temperature window), (d) Aufwärmen/Regenerieren core temperature, (e) Beförderung (transport) hygiene, (f) any maximum hot-holding time. Action: open https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2017/183/de and read the "Temperaturen"/"Lebensmittel" articles (roughly Art. 25–30 region) and the GVG guideline.

## A3. Liability if a shelter guest gets sick; any Swiss "Good Samaritan" protection; what BLV/Bundesrat have said (Aktionsplan 2022, Postulat Chevalley, Motion Munz, 2023–2024 items)

### Takeaway
Switzerland has **no** Good-Samaritan-style statute comparable to Italy's Legge Gadda, France's Loi Garot or the US Bill Emerson Act (none was found); the federal approach since the Aktionsplan of 6 April 2022 is to ease donations through targeted ordinance amendments (HyV Art. 29 Abs. 3, allergen simplification) and a guideline, not through a liability shield. Liability therefore follows general Swiss law (OR, PrHG) — the specific analysis is in the Bundesrat report on Postulat 22.3882 and the ZHAW 2024 paper, both unfetchable here.

### Cited Findings
- On 6 April 2022 the Bundesrat adopted the **Aktionsplan gegen die Lebensmittelverschwendung**, in fulfilment of **Postulat Chevalley 18.3829**; goal: halve avoidable food losses by 2030 vs 2017, set reduction targets with the sectors, prioritise measures by environmental impact; **14 measures**, among them "increase donation of unsold products" and "improve framework conditions for food donations (implementation of **Motion Munz 19.3112**)" — [Aktionsplan PDF, newsd.admin.ch](https://www.newsd.admin.ch/newsd/message/attachments/70975.pdf); [Bericht BR Postulat 22.3882](https://www.parlament.ch/centers/eparl/curia/2022/20223882/Bericht%20BR%20D.pdf) (snippets); overview [savefood.ch](https://savefood.ch/de/blog/detailseite/aktionsplan.html); ZHAW project on implementing the Aktionsplan [zhaw.ch](https://www.zhaw.ch/de/forschung/projekt/73498).
- A Bundesrat report on donations in retail exists as answer to **Postulat 22.3882** ("… und Detailhandel — Bericht des Bundesrates in Erfüllung …"); it records the Stretto 3 and Stretto 4 amendments described above — [parlament.ch PDF](https://www.parlament.ch/centers/eparl/curia/2022/20223882/Bericht%20BR%20D.pdf) (snippet).
- A further Bundesrat document dated **30 October 2024** (23 pages) appears in the same result set on food donations — [newsd.admin.ch 90242.pdf](https://www.newsd.admin.ch/newsd/message/attachments/90242.pdf) (title/content not retrievable).
- Bundesrat report on motions/postulates 2023 (BBl 2023 870) — [Fedlex BBl 2023 870](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/fga/2023/870/de) (content not retrieved).
- **Germany (for comparison only):** the BMEL guide states that for food given away free (or for a symbolic euro) donors are liable in tort only for intent or gross negligence (Schenkungsrecht), whereas paid transfer carries liability for any negligence; unsafe food can still trigger liability or criminal responsibility — [BMEL "Lebensmittel spenden" Leitfaden](https://www.bmleh.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Broschueren/lebensmittel-spenden.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3). EU Commission guidelines on food donation (2017) — [EUR-Lex 52017XC1025(01)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/DE/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:52017XC1025(01)).

### Inferences
- The Swiss donor's civil position is likely analogous to the German one: under Swiss Schenkungsrecht the donor answers to the donee only for intentional or grossly negligent damage (OR Art. 248 Abs. 1 — **verify on Fedlex SR 220**), while third parties (the shelter guest, who is not the donee) fall back on general tort law OR Art. 41 (fault-based) and possibly the Produktehaftpflichtgesetz (PrHG) — whether gratuitous hand-over is "in Verkehr gebracht" within the PrHG is exactly the point the ZHAW/Bundesrat texts should settle. **This is inference, not a finding.**
- For the platform the practical consequence is: written hand-over protocol (time, temperature, allergens, discard deadline, signatures) is the liability instrument that exists today; there is no statutory shield to point to.

### Gaps
- No Swiss parliamentary item found that *specifically* exempts donors from liability or VAT in 2023/2024 — only the general Aktionsplan and Postulat 22.3882 report; searching Curia Vista for "Lebensmittelspenden" 2023–2025 remains to be done (budget exhausted).
- Wording and status of **Motion Munz 19.3112** — not retrieved.
- Content of the 30 Oct 2024 Bundesrat document (90242.pdf) — not retrieved; could be the key 2024 statement.
- Swiss case law or BLV statement on donor liability for cooked meals — not found.

## A4. VAT (MWST) treatment of food donations; tax deductibility of in-kind donations by restaurants

### Takeaway
Under Swiss VAT, a gratuitous in-kind donation without advertising counter-performance obliges the donor to correct input tax or declare Eigenverbrauch once the in-kind value exceeds CHF 500 (per the GastroSuisse-area guidance found); below that threshold the gift is VAT-neutral. Income-tax deductibility for in-kind gifts to charities was not found in this run.

### Cited Findings
- "For in-kind donations (without a counter-service in the form of advertising), the donor must correct their input tax deduction or tax the product as self-consumption, if the in-kind service exceeds 500 Swiss francs" — search snippet attributed to the GastroSuisse VAT page — [GastroSuisse MWST im Gastgewerbe](https://gastrosuisse.ch/de/branchenwissen/unternehmensfuehrung/mwst) (snippet; attribution to this URL not 100 % certain — also see [swissvat.ch MI 05, 6 Feb 2025](https://www.swissvat.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/MI05_06.02.2025.pdf)).
- Two rates apply in gastronomy: 2.6 % for food handed over as goods, 8.1 % for restaurant service on site (2024/2025 rates) — [spesen-app.ch](https://spesen-app.ch/wiki/mwst-satz-restaurant-schweiz); [jamatu.ch](https://www.jamatu.ch/mwst-restaurant-schweiz).
- ESTV publishes VAT rulings for unclear cases — [ESTV MWST-Steuerpflicht](https://www.estv.admin.ch/estv/de/home/mehrwertsteuer/mwst-steuerpflicht.html).
- Austria (comparison): Austrian BMF has an explicit page on the tax treatment of food donations — [BMF Austria](https://www.bmf.gv.at/themen/steuern/fuer-unternehmen/umsatzsteuer/informationen/steuerliche-behandlung-von-lebensmittelspenden.html).

### Inferences
- The CHF 500 figure matches MWSTG Art. 31 Abs. 2 lit. c (Eigenverbrauch; gifts up to CHF 500 per person and year exempt) — **verify on Fedlex SR 641.20**. A restaurant giving a few kg of cooked food per evening to one organisation will exceed CHF 500/year quickly if valued at sales price; the valuation basis (cost vs. market value of unsaleable surplus, arguably near zero) is the decisive and unresolved question for the fact sheet.

### Gaps
- Whether Swiss surplus food donations to a tax-exempt organisation (DBG Art. 33a / Art. 59 Abs. 1 lit. c — **verify**) are deductible as in-kind gifts, and at what value — not found.
- ESTV MWST-Info or ruling specifically on *food* donations — not found; the Postulat 22.3882 report reportedly discusses VAT obstacles (snippet did not carry the content).

## A5. Allergen information duties when handing over unpackaged / repackaged food (14 allergens, written vs oral)

### Takeaway
For offen angebotene Lebensmittel the LIV allows either written allergen information or oral information by competent staff — but oral-only is permitted only with a clearly visible written notice and written documentation available to staff. For donated cooked meals passing through a second organisation, oral information does not travel, so a written allergen tag per container is the safe reading.

### Cited Findings
- For openly offered food, allergen information must be declared in writing (e.g., menu) **or** staff must be able to inform competently on request — [BLV, Informationen im Offenverkauf](https://www.blv.admin.ch/blv/de/home/lebensmittel-und-ernaehrung/lebensmittelsicherheit/naehrwertinformationen-und-kennzeichnung/offenverkauf.html) (snippet).
- Written declaration may be waived **only** if a clearly visible written notice states that information is given orally, and the information is available to staff in writing or a knowledgeable person can give it directly — [aha! Allergiezentrum Schweiz](https://www.aha.ch/aktuelles/offenverkauf-allergene-besser-deklarieren); [Kanton Graubünden Merkblatt Kennzeichnung offen in Verkehr gebrachter Lebensmittel (2019)](https://www.gr.ch/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dvs/alt/Documents/Kennzeichnung%20von%20offen%20in%20den%20Verkehr%20gebrachten%20Lebensmitteln%20de%20190226.pdf); [Rüttimann FoodSafety](https://www.ruettimann-foodsafety.ch/blog-allergene.html).
- Allergens covered include gluten-containing cereals, crustaceans, eggs, fish, soybeans, milk (incl. lactose), nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, lupins, molluscs, sulphur dioxide/sulphites (list rendered in snippet; peanuts also belong to the 14) — [BLV Offenverkauf](https://www.blv.admin.ch/blv/de/home/lebensmittel-und-ernaehrung/lebensmittelsicherheit/naehrwertinformationen-und-kennzeichnung/offenverkauf.html); BLV explanatory notes to the LIV — [BLV Erläuterungen LIV](https://www.blv.admin.ch/dam/blv/de/dokumente/lebensmittel-und-ernaehrung/rechts-und-vollzugsgrundlagen/lebensmittelrecht2017/erlaeuterung-verordnung-information-lebensmittel.pdf.download.pdf/12_1_Erl%C3%A4uterungen_zur_Verordnung_betreffend_die_Information_%C3%BCber_Lebensmittel_2.%C3%84K.pdf).
- Stretto 4 simplified allergen declaration for **baked goods** given to donation organisations (no equivalent for cooked meals mentioned) — [Bericht BR Postulat 22.3882](https://www.parlament.ch/centers/eparl/curia/2022/20223882/Bericht%20BR%20D.pdf) (snippet).

### Inferences
- A container of donated hot food that is re-served by a shelter is "offen abgegeben" twice; the only way the shelter can meet the oral-information standard is if the kitchen's written allergen list travels with the container — the platform's printed label should therefore carry the 14-allergen tick-list plus dish name, production time, and discard time.

### Gaps
- LIV article numbers (offen angebotene Lebensmittel; allergen Anhang) — **not verified** (Fedlex blocked; SR 817.022.16).
- Whether the Stretto 4 simplification extends beyond baked goods — not found.

## B2. United Against Waste measurements: waste per cover, composition, CHF per year

### Takeaway
UAW's field programme finds roughly 100–250 g of food waste per guest per restaurant visit, sets 50 g/guest as a good target, and reports average reductions of about 35 % after measurement-based coaching (one hotel: 129 → 44 g/guest, −66 %).

### Cited Findings
- Per guest per restaurant visit an estimated **100 to 250 g** of food waste arises — [SRF 1, "Food Waste – tausende von Franken landen im Müll"](https://www.srf.ch/radio-srf-1/food-waste-in-der-gastronomie-wenn-pures-geld-im-abfall-landet) (snippet).
- UAW method: staff record food waste daily for **four weeks**; measures are then defined; a success check follows **one year later** — [UAW, Schritt für Schritt weniger Food Waste](https://united-against-waste.ch/schritt-fuer-schritt-weniger-food-waste/).
- Example: at one hotel food waste per guest fell from ~129 g to 44 g between first and success measurement, −66 % — [UAW](https://united-against-waste.ch/schritt-fuer-schritt-in-eine-nachhaltige-zukunft/) (snippet).
- With expert support and UAW's "FSM" approach, businesses reduce food waste long-term by an average of **35 %** — [UAW](https://united-against-waste.ch/entscheidungen-die-den-unterschied-machen/) (snippet).
- Target of 50 g/guest named for one chef (Valentin Holenstein) — [Gastro Journal](https://gastrojournal.ch/home/gastronomie/gemeinsam-gegen-foodwaste) / [Pusch](https://www.pusch.ch/magazin/artikel/gemeinsam-potenziale-aufdecken) (snippet).
- GastroSuisse is founding and board member of UAW since 2013 — [GastroSuisse Food waste](https://gastrosuisse.ch/de/branchenwissen/nachhaltigkeit/food-waste).

### Inferences
- For a 60-cover evening, 100–250 g/cover implies 6–15 kg total food waste — but that total is dominated by plate waste and preparation waste, which are *not* redistributable; the redistributable "unserved, kept hot" fraction is a subset (see B4 gap).

- CHF value per kg / per year for a typical restaurant — SRF article title promises "tausende von Franken", figure not in snippet.
- Number of Betriebe measured by UAW — not found.

## B4. What fraction of surplus is realistically redistributable; redistribution platform averages (Too Good To Go, Tischlein deck dich, Schweizer Tafel)

### Takeaway
No Swiss study quantifying the *redistributable* (unserved, hot-held) fraction of restaurant surplus was found. The only throughput benchmarks are Too Good To Go Switzerland (≈ 5 700 partners, ≈ 2.5 M meals in 2022 ≈ 0.44 meals/partner/day) and Tischlein deck dich's distribution network.

### Cited Findings
- Too Good To Go Switzerland: **5 700 partners** (Jan 2023); **7 million** meals saved cumulatively, **about 2.5 million in 2022** — [Moneycab](https://www.moneycab.com/klima-umwelt/too-good-to-go-rund-7-millionen-gerettete-mahlzeiten/); [Horizont](https://www.horizont.net/schweiz/nachrichten/too-good-to-go-app-7-millionen-gerettete-mahlzeiten-205654); most meals saved in Zürich — [Presseportal](https://www.presseportal.ch/de/pm/100076742/100850797); bakery category ~1 meal per minute — [lebensmittelindustrie.com](https://www.lebensmittelindustrie.com/produktion/too-good-to-go-rettet-in-der-kategorie-backwaren-1-mahlzeit-pro-minute).
- Tischlein deck dich rescues food from destruction and distributes via its own Abgabestellen to people affected by poverty in CH and FL; **32 Abgabestellen per week in Kanton Zürich** — [tischlein.ch](https://www.tischlein.ch/); [Tischlein Region Ost](https://tischlein.ch/ueber-uns/regionen/ost); network article — [tischlein.ch news](https://tischlein.ch/de/ueber-uns/news-und-stories/lebensmittelrettung-schweiz-tischlein-deck-dich-im-netzwerk-n291).
- Schweizer Tafel collects donated surplus from food companies and distributes to people in need (mentioned in canton Zürich guidance) — [zh.ch](https://www.zh.ch/de/umwelt-tiere/nachhaltige-ernaehrung/nachhaltige-gastronomie.html) (snippet).
- Zero Waste Switzerland guide "Foodwaste vermeiden" (Sept 2024) — [PDF](https://zerowasteswitzerland.ch/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Guide_Foodwaste-DE.pdf).

### Inferences
- 2.5 M meals / 365 days / 5 700 partners ≈ **1.2 meals per partner per day** (using year-end partner count; ≈ 0.4/day if all partners were active all year at 5 700 — the true value lies between, because partner count grew during 2022). Order of magnitude: a typical TGTG partner sells one to two surprise bags per day.
- For hot cooked surplus (as opposed to bakery/retail), the redistributable share is structurally small: overproduction that was never on a plate and was kept ≥ 65 °C or rapidly chilled. Without a Swiss measurement, the fact sheet should present it as a range to be measured in the pilot, not as a national figure.

## C3. Insulated GN 1/1 thermoboxes: capacity, weight, how long ≥ 65 °C; data loggers

### Takeaway
EPP GN 1/1 thermoboxes (Thermo Future Box) cost roughly EUR 50–80 each, are 600 × 400 mm footprint with 21–37 L internal volume depending on height, and can be pre-heated with warming plates or chilled with cold packs; the manufacturer's stated retention curve was not retrievable — only one user report (~70 °C after 20 h) which should not be relied on.

### Cited Findings
- Thermo Future Box GN 1/1 Premium: external 600 × 400 × 230 mm; listing states 21 L in the title and "30-liter volume" in the description (conflicting); EPP (expanded polypropylene); can be heated with warming plates or cooled with cooling packs; one reviewer reports "good 70° over 20 hours" (anecdotal) — [Amazon.de listing](https://www.amazon.de/Thermo-Future-Box-expandiertes-Polypropylen/dp/B07B8YDGCG); [boxbar GN 1/1 H 230](https://www.boxbar.de/kueche-catering/aufbewahren-transportieren/thermotransportbehaelter/thermoboxen/5670/gn-thermobox-thermo-future-box-premium-gn-1/1-t-230-mm); [boxbar GN 1/1 H 400](https://www.boxbar.de/kueche-catering/aufbewahren-transportieren/thermotransportbehaelter/thermoboxen/5675/gn-thermobox-thermo-future-box-premium-gn-1/1-t-400-mm).
- Manufacturer prices (EUR, net): GN 1/1 basic 51.11; GN 1/1 Plus 61.82; GN 1/1 Digital 58.91; GN 1/1 Deluxe 76.76 (also a 37 L Deluxe); grey-blue 21 L 67.24; trolley/Fahrgestell 91.04 — [thermo-future-box.com](https://thermo-future-box.com/THERMOBOX-GN-1-1-DELUXE_1); [37 l](https://thermo-future-box.com/THERMOBOX-GN-1-1-DELUXE-37-l_1); [Digital](https://thermo-future-box.com/THERMOBOX-GN-1-1-DIGITAL); [Gastronorm 1/1](https://thermo-future-box.com/THERMOBOX-GASTRONORM-1-1_1); [Plus](https://thermo-future-box.com/THERMOBOX-GASTRONORM-1-1-PLUS_1); [Trolley](https://thermo-future-box.com/TROLLEY-THERMOBOX-GN-1-1).
- General hygiene guidance on cooling/heating in communal catering (German DGE source, comparative) — [Station Ernährung DGE](https://www.station-ernaehrung.de/fachinformationen/fuer-die-gemeinschaftsverpflegung/rund-um-die-gesetze/gute-hygienepraxis/kuehlen-gefrieren-und-erhitzen).

### Inferences
- A GN 1/1 H 230 box holds one GN 1/1 × 200 mm or two GN 1/1 × 100 mm containers; at ~3.5–4 L of stew per GN 1/1 × 100 mm and ~350 g portions, one box ≈ **20–25 portions**, weight ≈ 8–10 kg loaded (EPP box ~1.5–2 kg — weight not confirmed). Three boxes in a long-john front box ≈ 60–75 portions ≈ 25–30 kg, well under payload.
- Heat retention: EPP boxes loaded with food at ≥ 75 °C and pre-warmed typically stay above 65 °C for a few hours; the ~20 h reviewer figure is not credible as a design value. Design the round so that pick-up → delivery is ≤ 2 h and the temperature is logged at both ends.
