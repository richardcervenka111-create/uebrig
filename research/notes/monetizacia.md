## Key question 1 — Business models actually used in the segment, with revenue numbers

### Takeaway
Only two model families demonstrably reach financial self-sufficiency: (a) consumer marketplaces that take a per-transaction cut (Too Good To Go: CHF 2.90 per bag + CHF 59/year in Switzerland; DKK 725 m revenue and a small profit in 2024) and (b) B2B fee-for-service / SaaS (Copia, Goodr, Phenix, Winnow/Kitro, Olio's paid Food Waste Heroes). Every pure redistribution-to-charity organisation found (Tischlein deck dich, Schweizer Tafel, Partage, FareShare, foodsharing, Tafel, MealConnect) is donation/grant financed, and the retail-of-surplus models are fragile (Sirplus insolvency 2024) unless at large scale (Motatos €87 m).

### Inferences
- Goods value per kg at the two Swiss redistributors is ~CHF 6.5–7.3/kg (55.4 m / 8,530 t; 55.6 m / 7,655 t), so "value of food saved" headlines are 10–20× larger than the money actually flowing through these organisations — do not confuse the two when sizing Übrig Bern's impact claims.
- The only Swiss same-day, restaurant-facing surplus channel with a proven price point is Too Good To Go at CHF 2.90 + CHF 59/year. A charitable platform cannot charge the consumer (the recipient is a social organisation), so the analogous "per transfer" fee would have to be paid by the restaurant or a third party.
- Olio is the closest analogue to Übrig Bern (surplus → volunteers/charities, for-profit, corporate fees) and even with Tesco nationally its fees cover only ~30 % of overheads; the rest is investor money. A one-city version should not expect restaurant fees alone to cover costs.
- Karma's and Sirplus's trajectories (pivot to B2B hardware; insolvency of the retail model) point to the same lesson: the money is in the B2B side (fees, SaaS, waste-bill savings), not in moving the food itself.

## Key question 2 — Which revenue streams are realistic for a one-city platform

### Takeaway
Evidence from comparable organisations supports four streams for Bern: (1) foundation/corporate sponsorship (the way Tischlein, Schweizer Tafel and Madame Frigo actually fund themselves — Coop, Migros, Ernst Göhner Stiftung, Electrolux, Aldi); (2) small fixed fees from restaurants (the CHF 59/year Too Good To Go precedent shows Swiss gastro accepts a low admin fee); (3) municipal/cantonal money, which exists but is small and uneven (Zug CHF 25,000 for 10 fridges; Geneva's canton subsidy to Partage reported as tiny; Schweizer Tafel gets none); (4) licensing the platform to other cities, for which the searched evidence is only indirect. Donation receipts for food (Sachspenden) are legally possible but of near-zero value for perishable surplus.

- Restaurants already accept a CHF 59 annual admin fee plus CHF 2.90 per bag in Switzerland; associations negotiate first-year waivers — [CafetierSuisse](https://cafetier.ch/too-good-to-go/)
- Corporate-partner cash: Coop gives ~CHF 425,000/year plus ~7,200 t of food to Tischlein deck dich and partners — [Taten statt Worte](https://www.taten-statt-worte.ch/de/unsere-taten/tat-nr-105.html)
- Foundation channel: Tischlein deck dich has a dedicated "Stiftungen" fundraising page; Ernst Göhner Stiftung is a long-term partner of both Tischlein and Schweizer Tafel — [tischlein.ch Stiftungen](https://www.tischlein.ch/geld-spenden/stiftungen/); [schweizertafel.ch](https://schweizertafel.ch/de/)
- Church money: Katholische Landeskirche gave CHF 25,000 each to Tischlein deck dich and Soliladen Sursee out of a surplus — [headtopics/Kath. Landeskirche](https://ch.headtopics.com/news/300-000-franken-aus-uberschuss-katholische-landeskirche-88016483)

- B2B fee logic used by Copia/Goodr: the client pays because the service yields a tax deduction plus lower waste bills, and the platform supplies the audit-ready records — [Copia FAQ](https://gocopia.com/faq-support/); [Supermarket News on Goodr](https://www.supermarketnews.com/grocery-technology/goodr-wants-to-stop-food-waste)

### Inferences
- Restaurant subscription: a CHF 5–10/month or CHF 59–120/year fee is within the price point Swiss gastro already pays TGTG; but TGTG also *pays the restaurant* (bag sales), whereas Übrig only removes a cost. Expect lower willingness to pay; position the fee as "documented donation + waste-bill saving + label", not as a marketplace.
- Spendenbescheinigung for food: legally possible (Naturalspende, since 2007), but same-day surplus has near-zero Verkehrswert and the donor needs evidence of value; the deduction is negligible for a restaurant. Treat the receipt as a goodwill/ESG artefact, not as a monetisable benefit (unlike the US enhanced deduction Copia/Goodr rely on).
- Sponsorship by Coop/Migros/regional banks is the empirically dominant Swiss funding route, but the two national organisations already hold those relationships; a Bern-only platform would compete for the same donors unless positioned as complementary (hot/same-day meals from restaurants, which neither Tischlein nor Tafel handle).
- Municipality: Stadt Bern precedent for financing this specific activity was not found; the realistic municipal asks are small project grants (Zug-style CHF 25,000 range) or in-kind support (Kompetenzzentrum Arbeit placements as drivers), not a service contract.
- Licensing to other cities is plausible in principle (MealConnect is a nationally funded platform; Madame Frigo scaled through per-fridge activation fees) but no Swiss city-to-city SaaS precedent was found.
- Zewo is unaffordable and unnecessary in year 1 (CHF 7,500+ up front, plus annual licence); tax exemption (Steuerbefreiung) is the cheaper credibility marker foundations actually require.
- Advertising: excluded by the founder; no evidence any peer uses it.

## Key question 3 — Swiss legal forms: Verein vs Stiftung vs GmbH/AG vs "gemeinnützige GmbH", tax exemption, ZEWO, hybrids, salaries, liability, cost, exit

### Takeaway
The Verein is the cheapest, fastest and most common form for Swiss food redistribution (Tischlein deck dich, Madame Frigo, foodsharing are Vereine; Schweizer Tafel and Partage are Stiftungen); a "gemeinnützige GmbH" does not exist as a legal form but a GmbH can be drafted to charitable standards and, in some cantons, tax-exempted. Tax exemption (gemeinnützig) requires Allgemeininteresse, an open beneficiary circle, no Erwerbszweck and traditionally an unpaid board — which is the single biggest constraint for a founder who wants a salary or a later sale.

### Cited Findings
- Kanton Bern (TaxInfo) conditions for Steuerbefreiung wegen Gemeinnützigkeit: activity in the general interest; open circle of beneficiaries (offers limited to members, family or one profession are not charitable); no self-interest — "cannot strive for profitability, acquisition or profit-making"; the entity must make "significant sacrifices", meaning the board works on a volunteer basis — [Kanton Bern TaxInfo Steuerbefreiung juristischer Personen](https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/01052ba2-c59b-4ac3-ad3f-db3143931ae3); [Kanton Bern TaxInfo Steuerbefreiung beantragen](https://www.taxinfo.sv.fin.be.ch/taxinfo/c3a6ac1c-b808-458d-b980-e53c8ad4b299)
- The federal reference is ESTV Kreisschreiben Nr. 12 (8 July 1994) on tax exemption and deductibility of donations — [Kreisschreiben Nr. 12 (hosted by Kanton Zürich)](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/juristischepersonen/steuerbefreite-institutionen/kreisschreiben_nr12_vom_08-07-1994_estv.pdf); [Tier im Recht summary](https://www.tierimrecht.org/documents/251/gk_steuerbefreiung_de.pdf)
- Board pay: the board must be ehrenamtlich, or in some cantons (ZH, BS, AG, VD named) may receive an "angemessene Entschädigung", the amount varying by canton — [Clubdesk](https://www.clubdesk.ch/de/vereinswissen/steuern-verein-schweiz); a BDO survey of cantonal tax administrations finds board compensation "has for years no longer been the decisive criterion", with large cantonal differences — [BDO](https://www.bdo.ch/de-ch/publikationen/entschadigung-trotz-ehrenamtlichkeit-(teil-1)); [FFHS](https://www.ffhs.ch/en/ueber-uns/news/article/2024-05-01-ehrenamt-und-bezahlung-ein-widerspruch)
- A Geschäftsführer of a gemeinnütziger Verein can be salaried, provided the pay is consistent with charitable principles (market-appropriate, not disguised profit distribution) — [Bernhard Assekuranz Vereinswissen](https://bernhard-assekuranz.com/vereinswissen/vereinsfuehrung/zahlungen-an-vereinsmitglieder-und-vorstaende/)
- Board liability: board members are personally liable to the Verein, its members and third parties if they breach their duties — [Organhaftung Schweiz](https://organhaftung-schweiz.ch/persoenliche-haftung-eines-vereinsvorstandes/)
- Verein founding: by an assembly of two persons plus written statutes; quick and cheap; no minimum capital — [Vereinsplaner](https://vereinsplaner.at/c/verein-gruenden-in-der-schweiz); [vitamin B Rechtsformen](https://www.vitaminb.ch/vereinsthemen/gruendung/rechtsformen)
- Stiftung founding: minimum capital CHF 50,000; public notarisation; "much more resources" — [Goldblum](https://goldblum.ch/de/wissensdatenbank/stiftungen-vereine-und-verein-nach-schweizerischem-recht); [SwissFoundations](https://www.swissfoundations.ch/stiftungspraxis/stiftungsgruendung/); [startups.ch](https://blog.startups.ch/verein-oder-gemeinnuetzige-stiftung-welches-ist-die-passendere-rechtsform-fuer-mein-vorhaben/)
- GmbH founding (2025/26): one-off costs CHF 1,400–9,200 plus CHF 20,000 Stammkapital (stays in the company); notary CHF ~500 (Aargau) to ~3,000 (Geneva); Handelsregister federal fee CHF 600 base + CHF 30 per signatory + CHF 20 per function; realistic total CHF 2,500–5,000 plus capital — [pfeffersack.ch](https://pfeffersack.ch/blog/was-kostet-gmbh-gruendung-schweiz); [newco.ch](https://newco.ch/blog/gmbh-gruenden--26); [startups.ch](https://www.startups.ch/en/establish-a-llc/how-much-does-it-cost-to-set-up-a-llc-in-switzerland)
- "Gemeinnützige GmbH": Swiss law, unlike German law, has no such legal form; the GmbH is conceived as a commercial, profit-oriented body; in practice a GmbH can be structured to pursue charitable purposes and under conditions obtain tax exemption — [Dufour Advokatur (10 Nov 2025)](https://www.dufour-advokatur.ch/2025/11/10/die-gemeinnuetzige-gmbh-in-der-schweiz/)
- Legal forms in the sector: Tischlein deck dich = Verein ("Verein Tischlein deck dich") — [fundraiso](https://www.fundraiso.com/en/organisations/verein-tischlein-deck-dich); Schweizer Tafel = Stiftung with a separate Gönnerverein as fundraising vehicle — [Gönnerverein Schweizer Tafel](https://goennerverein-schweizertafel.ch/); Partage = Fondation — [partage.ch](https://www.partage.ch/en/); Madame Frigo = gemeinnütziger Verein — [Blick](https://www.blick.ch/life/essen/coole-idee-gegen-foodwaste-darum-stellen-wir-kuehlschraenke-auf-die-strasse-id17884574.html); Äss-Bar = GmbH (commercial) — [Moneyhouse](https://www.moneyhouse.ch/en/company/aess-bar-gmbh-7008908291); Too Good To Go = ApS (Danish Ltd) — [CVR filing](https://regnskaber.cvrapi.dk/40316573/amNsb3VkczovLzAzL2M0L2EyLzY5LzY5LzRkMTgtNDg5OS1hMzliLTQ1NzRiMmIzMmUwNw.pdf); Olio = UK Ltd — [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/09428660/officers)
- Zewo: seal granted for five years after Vorprüfung + Hauptprüfung; annual licence scaled to size — [Zewo Der Weg zum Gütesiegel](https://www.zewo.ch/Dokumente/Unterlagen-fur-Zewo-Zertifizierung/Der-Weg-zum-Gutesiegel.pdf); [Die 21 Zewo-Standards](https://zewo.ch/de/die-21-zewo-standards/)

### Inferences
- For Übrig Bern the practical decision tree is: **Verein with Steuerbefreiung** if the goal is donations/foundation grants and the founder is content with a salaried Geschäftsführer role (not a paid board seat) and no personal equity; **GmbH** if the goal is a sellable software asset with paid licences to other cities and no reliance on charitable money; **hybrid (Verein/Stiftung owns or contracts a GmbH)** if both are wanted — the Schweizer Tafel "Stiftung + Gönnerverein" split shows Swiss practice already separates fundraising and operating vehicles. The hybrid's mechanics (Verein licenses software from founder's GmbH at arm's length) were not confirmed by a fetched source and need a tax adviser's check.
- Kanton Bern's TaxInfo wording ("board must work on a volunteer basis") is stricter than the cantons BDO lists as allowing compensation; assume Bern requires an unpaid board unless the Steuerverwaltung confirms otherwise.
- Exit: a tax-exempt Verein's assets are purpose-bound and cannot be paid out to founders or members on dissolution (this is the standard Liquidationsklausel demanded for exemption — see Gaps for the unfetched text); therefore a "sale" is impossible in the Verein form, whereas a GmbH's shares are freely sellable. A Stiftung is the least exit-capable of all (irrevocable dedication, state supervision).
- Costs and time: Verein ≈ CHF 0 and one evening (statutes + founding assembly), Handelsregister entry optional unless it runs a commercial business; GmbH ≈ CHF 2,500–5,000 + CHF 20,000 capital and a few weeks; Stiftung ≈ CHF 50,000 capital + notary + supervision.

- Kanton Bern specific practice on Vorstandsentschädigung limits, Gesuch documents, processing time and fee — TaxInfo page blocked; only the summary snippet above.

## Key question 4 — Unit economics reference points

### Takeaway
The one hard Swiss efficiency figure found is Schweizer Tafel's: every donated franc moves 1.81 kg of goods worth CHF 11.80 — i.e., a redistribution cost of ~CHF 0.55 per kg. Biowaste disposal in the Bern region is cited at CHF 60–120 per tonne, meaning a restaurant saves only ~6–12 Rappen per kg by donating rather than binning — too small to be the restaurant's motive or the platform's revenue base.

### Cited Findings
- Schweizer Tafel: per donated franc, 1.81 kg of goods worth CHF 11.80 are distributed — [schweizertafel.ch Spendenfranken](https://schweizertafel.ch/spendenfranken/)
- Schweizer Tafel 2024: 7,655 t rescued (+468 t taken over), ~30 t/day, goods value CHF 55.6 m — [Diakonie Schweiz](https://diakonie.ch/schweizer-tafel-hat-2024-ueber-8000-tonnen-ueberschuessige-lebensmittel-verteilt/)
- Tischlein deck dich 2024: 8,530 t, goods value CHF 55.4 m, 160+ Abgabestellen, 4,500+ volunteers — [SWI swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/tischlein-deck-dich-rettet-7200-tonnen-lebensmittel/71468117)
- Partage 2025: 3,760 t → ~7 m meals (≈0.54 kg/meal as used by Partage) — [partage.ch](https://www.partage.ch/fr/actualites/a-geneve-plus-de-17000-personnes-par-semaine-ont-eu-recours-a-laide-alimentaire-en-2025)
- FareShare 2023/24: 56,000 t; later "135 million meals" — [FareShare news](https://fareshare.org.uk/news-media/news/fareshare-publishes-annual-report-celebrating-30-years-of-strengthening-communities/)
- Biowaste price in the Bern area: "between 60 and 120 Swiss francs per tonne" referenced for ARA Bern (article on competition for biowaste depressing eco-power prices; date not in snippet) — [Der Bund](https://www.derbund.ch/bern/Kampf-um-begehrte-BioAbfaelle-drueckt-koStrompreise/story/26028947)
- Recycling Energie AG serves 2,000+ gastronomy businesses in German-speaking Switzerland, processing ~40,000 t/year of food waste, trimmings, manure and green waste into ~18 m kWh — [Recycling Energie Gastronomie](https://www.recycling-energie.ch/dienstleistungen/gastronomie/); [Recycling Energie Ökostrom](https://www.recycling-energie.ch/produkte/oekostrom/)
- Stadt Bern: household Grüngut collection has accepted food scraps since 1 Jan 2015, processed at the Seeland plant into biogas and compost (household service, not gastro) — [Stadt Bern Mediencenter](https://www.bern.ch/mediencenter/medienmitteilungen/aktuell_ptk/gruengutsammlung-neu-auch-fuer-ruestabfaelle-und-speisereste); Entsorgungshof minimum fee CHF 2.00 per fraction — [Stadt Bern Tarife](https://www.bern.ch/politik-und-verwaltung/stadtverwaltung/tvs/entsorgung-recycling-stadt-bern-erb/gebuehren-und-tarife/tarife-entsorgungshoefe)
- Volunteer valuation (BFS Satellitenkonto 2020): 621 m hours of volunteering (454 m informal, 166 m institutional); all unpaid work valued at CHF 434 bn, of which volunteering ≈ 8 %; valuation method = average labour cost of comparable market occupations — [BFS press release 2020](https://www.bfs.admin.ch/bfs/en/home/statistics/catalogues-databases.assetdetail.23767760.html); [BFS Freiwilligenarbeit](https://www.bfs.admin.ch/bfs/de/home/statistiken/querschnittsthemen/wohlfahrtsmessung/aktivitaeten/unbezahlte-arbeit/freiwilligenarbeit.html)
- Consumer-app economics for a restaurant: ResQ example 600 portions/month → €2,500/month extra income (≈ €4.2 per portion net to restaurant after the 25 % commission) — [ResQ Club](https://www.resq-club.com/sell/cases/konnichiwa)
- Too Good To Go in Switzerland takes CHF 2.90 per bag; the restaurant keeps the remainder of the bag price — [CafetierSuisse](https://cafetier.ch/too-good-to-go/)
- Winnow claims 2–8 % food-cost savings within 12 months for kitchens that measure waste — [Winnow](https://www.winnowsolutions.com/)

### Inferences
- Cost per kg redistributed at Schweizer Tafel ≈ CHF 0.55 (1 / 1.81); at ~0.5 kg per meal that is ≈ CHF 0.28 per meal, achieved with a national truck fleet, volunteers and Zivis. Implied annual cash cost at 7,655 t ≈ CHF 4.2 m *if* the per-franc ratio holds for 2024 (year of the ratio not stated).
- Volunteer hour value from BFS: ~8 % × CHF 434 bn ≈ CHF 35 bn / 621 m h ≈ CHF 56 per hour (order of magnitude; BFS publishes the exact per-hour figures by activity, not read here). This is the number to use when valuing in-kind volunteer time in a grant application.
- Restaurant's avoided disposal cost at CHF 60–120/t = CHF 0.06–0.12/kg; a 5 kg daily surplus saves CHF 0.30–0.60/day — irrelevant as a purchase motive. The restaurant's real motives are staff morale, ESG/Label communication and not having to throw food away, which argues for a flat, low fee and strong reporting rather than a per-kg fee.
- Übrig Bern's marginal cost per transfer is close to zero (recipient picks up; founder's software on free tiers); the binding costs are founder time, food-safety liability handling and eventually a coordinator — so the relevant unit is "cost per active restaurant per month", not per kg.

## Key question 5 — City-level food-rescue platforms that reached sustainability or failed, and why

### Takeaway
No Swiss city-level surplus platform was found that reached self-financing; the sustainable Swiss examples are national donation-funded logistics organisations (Tischlein, Tafel) or micro-infrastructure Vereine with per-unit activation fees and municipal co-operators (Madame Frigo). The failure and pivot cases found (Sirplus, Karma) share high fixed costs and margins too thin to survive on moving food; the survivors monetise B2B fees, data or a consumer cut.

### Cited Findings
- Madame Frigo scaled nationally as a Verein by selling fridge "hardware" against an activation fee, having operators (municipalities, parishes, companies, individuals) pay electricity and cleaning, local volunteers monitor, Electrolux supply fridges, and cantons/municipalities fund batches (Zug CHF 25,000 for 10) — [Blick](https://www.blick.ch/life/essen/coole-idee-gegen-foodwaste-darum-stellen-wir-kuehlschraenke-auf-die-strasse-id17884574.html); [zentralplus](https://www.zentralplus.ch/madame-frigo-geld-fuer-zehn-neue-kuehlschraenke-im-kanton-zug-2094195/); [Gemeinde Wald ZH](https://www.wald-zh.ch/dienstleistungen/94817); [Burgdorf im Wandel](https://burgdorf-im-wandel.ch/portfolio/madamefrigo/)
- Sirplus (Berlin): insolvency January 2024; warehouse closed, team from ~90 to <30; "high fixed costs, low margins; subscription models and new products did not grow fast enough"; restarted with reduced team after brand was secured by co-founder + investors — [Handelsblatt](https://www.handelsblatt.com/unternehmen/handel-konsumgueter/insolvenz-lebensmittelretter-sirplus-benoetigt-eigene-rettung/100006045.html); [retail-news](https://retail-news.de/insolvenz-abgewendet-re-start-fuer-berliner-online-supermarkt-sirplus/); [Wirtschaftsjournal](https://wirtschaftsjournal.com/sirplus-nach-insolvenz-gerettet-neustart-mit-reduziertem-team/)
- Karma: pivoted from a deals platform into restaurant surplus (2015/16), raised $12 m (2018), then moved into smart fridges — [Medium/Foundersuite](https://medium.com/foundersuite/good-karma-how-a-swedish-mission-centric-food-app-pivoted-its-way-to-a-12m-series-a-round-de23749add49); [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/karma-food-waste-smart-fridge)
- Olio: even with Tesco as national partner, corporate fees cover ~30 % of overheads — [Olio programme pages](https://olioapp.com/business/food-waste-heroes-programme/)
- SRF framed Swiss food-waste apps as "a drop on a hot stone" relative to total waste — [SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/verschwendung-von-essen-foodwaste-apps-sind-nur-ein-tropfen-auf-den-heissen-stein); swissinfo covered Swiss food-startup failures in general — [SWI](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/agrarwirtschaft/wenn-schweizer-food-startups-scheitern/90918013)
- New Swiss entrant GoNina (AI demand forecasts + discounted surprise packages for businesses) shows the segment still attracts for-profit B2B/marketplace hybrids — [Presseportal Schweiz](https://www.presseportal-schweiz.ch/pressemeldungen/gonina-die-neue-schweizer-plattform-bekaempft-foodwaste-ki)
- US city model: 412 Food Rescue (Pittsburgh) is a volunteer-driven, app-based city food rescue non-profit still scaling in 2025 — [Axios Pittsburgh](https://www.axios.com/local/pittsburgh/2025/10/29/412-food-rescue-pittsburgh-food-waste-snap); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/412_Food_Rescue); Food Rescue US runs a similar volunteer app model — [Food Rescue US](https://foodrescue.us/about/faqs/)
- ReFED: obstacles to efficient food rescue are "aggravating but eminently solvable" (logistics, liability, matching) — [ReFED](https://refed.org/articles/the-obstacles-to-more-efficient-food-rescue-and-donation-are-aggravating--but-eminently-solvable/)

### Inferences
- The Madame Frigo pattern is the most transferable to a one-founder Bern platform: keep the central Verein tiny, push operating cost to the operators (here: restaurants and recipient organisations), charge a one-off activation/onboarding fee, and let municipalities/cantons fund "batches" (e.g., onboarding of 10 restaurants) rather than operations.
- 412 Food Rescue is the closest precedent for a city platform that licenses its software to other cities (Food Rescue Hero) — but its budget and licence terms were not verifiable this session; treat as a hypothesis to check.
- A Swiss restaurant-surplus-to-charity platform that shut down was not found; absence of failures may reflect absence of attempts (Too Good To Go covers the consumer side; Tischlein/Tafel cover retail surplus; same-day hot meals from restaurants to social organisations appear to be an uncovered niche in Bern).

- Any Swiss or DACH city-level restaurant-surplus-to-charity platform that failed, with reasons — not found.
- Restessbar (Winterthur) and Foodsharing Schweiz organisational costs — not found.
- Partage's evolution from Geneva Fondation to a canton-supported food bank (income mix over time) — 2023 report blocked.
