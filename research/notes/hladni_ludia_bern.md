## KQ1 — Numbers: poverty, Sozialhilfe, Nothilfe, homelessness, students, pensioners (Stadt/Kanton Bern)

### Takeaway
Kanton Bern had 38,790 people on Sozialhilfe in 2024 (quota 3.66 %, falling), but the Städteinitiative's 2024 city comparison singles out the **City of Bern as showing a "deutliche Zunahme" of Sozialhilfe recipients**, and the city's own count of street-homeless people more than doubled from 21 (2021) to 48 (2024) and 56 (2025). Roughly 300 rejected asylum seekers live on CHF 10/day Nothilfe in cantonal Rückkehrzentren (one of them underground in Bern-Brünnen), more than 100 of them children.

### Inferences
- The street-homeless count (56) understates the evening-meal demand relevant to a shelter route: the Notschlafstellen listed in KQ3 alone have ~20 (Sleeper) + 50–60 (Passantenheim) + 7 (Pluto) + 20 seasonal (Tiefenau/successor) beds, and Punkt 6 and the Gassenküche serve non-residents too.
- The Nothilfe population (CHF 10/day, ~300 people, >100 children) is the group with the sharpest structural food gap, but it sits mostly outside the city (Aarwangen, Enggistein, Gampelen, Biel) except Bern-Brünnen and the private-hosting network — a same-evening restaurant route would reach it only via the Solidaritätsnetz/St. Marien Mittagstisch (lunch) or Brünnen.

## KQ2 — Food insecurity in Switzerland (national context only)

### Takeaway
BFS 2024: 8.4 % income-poor (≈743,000 people) and 6.0 % materially and socially deprived; Caritas counts 1.34 million poor or at risk (15 %). Demand at Caritas-Märkte hit a third consecutive record in 2024 (>1.1 million sales) and Schweizer Tafel distributed 7,655 t (+18 %) to 511 institutions — about 45 of them in the Bern region.

## KQ3 — Bern institutions: address, days, hours, capacity, operator, contact, food source

### Takeaway
The city has a small, well-defined set of low-threshold food/shelter points. Only three serve **hot evening food**: Sleeper (Gassenküche, daily 18–20), Pluto (dinner for 7 youths) and Passantenheim (Halbpension). Punkt 6 (18:00–22:30/23:00 in winter) and the Passantenhilfe Aufenthaltsraum are the drop-in points. Nearly all cook from Schweizer Tafel deliveries.

## KQ6 — Past attempts in Bern to bring restaurant surplus to shelters

### Takeaway
No documented Bern scheme moving **restaurant** surplus to shelters was found. What exists is retail→Tafel→institution (Sleeper, La Prairie, St. Marien), a restaurant↔Tafel loop at Kursaal Bern (the Kursaal *receives* Tafel fruit to process, ~2.5–3.5 t/yr), consumer apps (Too Good To Go ≈17,000 meals in Kanton Bern in an early year), and public fridges.

### Cited Findings
- Kursaal Bern cooperates with Schweizer Tafel: the Tafel delivers ripe fruit in bulk that it cannot redistribute; Kursaal pays the food value and processes it into sauces/preserves; ~3.2 t rescued 2024, >2.5 t 2025, 3.5 t in an earlier year — [kursaal-bern.ch story](https://kursaal-bern.ch/ueber-uns/stories/32-tonnen-lebensmittel-gerettet); [kursaal-bern.ch Nachhaltigkeit](https://kursaal-bern.ch/ueber-uns/nachhaltigkeit/essen-trinken). (Direction is Tafel → restaurant, not restaurant → shelter.)
- Too Good To Go: "App rettet fast 17'000 Mahlzeiten im Kanton Bern" — [Der Bund](https://www.derbund.ch/bern/app-rettet-fast-17000-mahlzeiten-im-kanton-bern/story/22796854) (date not in snippet; consumer sale, not donation).
- Sleeper takes surplus from "regional food retailers" as food-waste avoidance — [sleeper.ch](https://sleeper.ch/).
- United Against Waste writes on cooperation in large kitchens (Kursaal case) — [united-against-waste.ch](https://united-against-waste.ch/kooperation-statt-kompromiss-im-grossbetrieb/).
- offene kirche bern runs a "Foodsave" offer — [offene-kirche.ch](https://www.offene-kirche.ch/angebote-veranstaltungen/spirittuell-kulturell/foodsave) (nature of the food and its source not retrieved).

### Inferences
- The absence of any hot-food restaurant→shelter precedent in the Bern press is itself a finding: the platform would be first-of-kind locally, and the Lebensmittelkontrolle Bern (which already inspects Sleeper) is the regulator to engage before the first handover.

- Whether any Bern restaurant or hotel has an informal arrangement with Sleeper/Passantenheim (would need direct interviews).
- Basel's Gassenküche donation rules could be a template but were not confirmed as Bern practice.
